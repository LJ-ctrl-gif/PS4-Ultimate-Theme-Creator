Packaging tools
===============

THIS FOLDER SHIPS EMPTY IN THE REPOSITORY. The binaries are proprietary Sony
software and are not redistributed here; you have to obtain them separately
and lay them out like this:

  tools/
    orbis-pub-cmd.exe     builds param.sfo and the package image (img_create)
    at9tool.exe           optional: converts the music to ATRAC9
    ext/
      sc.exe              orbis-pub-cmd calls it; without it, it won't start
      di.exe              same
      libatrac9.dll       needed to measure the loudness of an .at9

The app works without them: on startup it detects they are missing, disables
the Package tab on its own and everything else keeps working. Without
at9tool.exe the music has to be uploaded already converted to .at9.

Where they come from
--------------------

orbis-pub-cmd.exe, sc.exe and di.exe come from "PS4 Fake PKG Tools 3.87
(Patched) V7", packaged by CyB1K, with credit to flat_z, cfwprophet,
SocraticBliss, TheRadziu, Arczi and Opoisso. It is Sony Interactive
Entertainment Inc.'s "Publishing Tools ver. 3.87" -- proprietary SDK software
with binary patches applied by the scene (Custom PKG Key, Fake SELF Support,
Online Checks Patched). It is not free software and there is no public source,
which is why the app shells out to it instead of reimplementing it.

at9tool.exe is the "SCEI ATRAC9 TOOL", also from Sony's official SDK.

Why only these
--------------

They are the minimum set. Verified empirically: the package they produce is
byte-for-byte identical to the one from the full 16 MB distribution. The rest
(the gengp4/orbis-pub-gen GUIs, the orbis-pub-chk validator, the trophy
utilities and the PSX-PS2-FPKG folder) is unused.

Heads-up: orbis-pub-chk.exe hangs without producing any output. Don't waste
time on it.
